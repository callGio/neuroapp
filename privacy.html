Privacy

Effective 13 November 2026

Gyrafold is a free app for learning the human brain. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No cookies.
  • The app does not send anything about you to a server. It only downloads its own files (pages, lessons and 3D models) from gyrafold.com.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress, your chosen level and a few display settings are saved only in your browser’s local storage, on this device. The keys start with neuroapp.. They never leave your device.

On the welcome page, the browser’s session storage remembers that Oron (the guide) has already said hello, so the greeting does not repeat. It is cleared when you close the tab.

So that Gyrafold can work offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the app’s own files, including the 3D models, on this device.

How to erase it

To erase quiz progress only, open the whole-brain atlas, go to the Progress tab and choose Reset progress.

To erase everything, clear this site’s data (also called site storage or website data) in your browser’s settings. If you installed Gyrafold as an app, uninstalling it may also remove it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain name is looked up through Cloudflare DNS. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when your browser downloads the site, and Cloudflare may log the domain lookups. Gyrafold has no access to those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Links to other sites, such as the source code on GitHub, take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

Children

Gyrafold collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
