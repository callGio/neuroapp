## Description (long)

Gyrafold is a free app for learning the human brain.

Rotate a 3D model of the brain, click any part to see its name and where it sits, and read short lessons on what brain regions do. The models come from the SPL/NAC Brain Atlas (Halle et al., 2017), and the lessons cite their sources. Pick your level, from new to the subject to expert, and change it at any time.

Guided tours walk through how regions work together. Quizzes ask you to find or name structures and use spaced review, so the parts you are about to forget come back first. Your progress is saved on your device.

Two more sections go beyond the whole brain. Neuron mode zooms in to a single nerve cell: click its parts and watch a signal travel down the axon and across a synapse. Brain imaging explains what MRI, DTI, CT, fMRI, PET, EEG and MEG each measure, and what they cannot tell you.

Gyrafold is for students, teachers and anyone curious about the brain.

It is free, with no account, no ads, no purchases and no tracking. Once the 3D atlas has finished loading one time while online, Gyrafold works offline.

Educational use only; not medical advice.

## Features (6)

- A 3D brain atlas with more than 300 structures you can rotate, zoom, slice with cutting planes and isolate.
- Short lessons at beginner and expert levels, with their sources cited.
- Guided tours of how brain regions work together.
- Find-it and name-it quizzes with spaced review; progress stays on your device.
- Neuron mode: the parts of a nerve cell, and how a signal travels and crosses a synapse.
- Brain imaging: what MRI, DTI, CT, fMRI, PET, EEG and MEG measure, compared side by side.
